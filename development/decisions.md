# Development Decisions

Record explicit resolutions here before or alongside promotion into the canonical grammar.

## Decision protocol

1. State the question.
2. Record the alternatives considered.
3. Record the selected analysis only after explicit decision.
4. Update the relevant `canon/` or domain file.
5. Move the unresolved item out of `open-questions.md` when resolved.

## Decisions recorded 2026-09-17

### Phonology

1. **Vowel inventory:** synchronic vowels are `/a e i u/`; `/o/` is excluded.
2. **Consonant inventory:** synchronic consonants are `/p t k c m n s h w j r/`, with moderate conditioned/allophonic variation to be specified.
3. **Palatalization:** `t` and `k` become `c` before `/i/`.
4. **`ndt` development:** the attested historical development to gemination/simplification is treated as historical/lexicalized rather than a productive synchronic rule.
5. **Prohibited sequences:** `wu → u`; `yi → ye`.
6. **Stress:** stress is predictable and weight-sensitive: rightmost heavy syllable, otherwise penultimate. The precise definition of heavy remains open.

### Morphology

7. **Applicatives:** dedicated verbal `-sen-` and `-mer-` applicatives are eliminated. Instrumental and benefactive meanings remain available through the nominal case system.
8. **AUX/DERIV:** `-re-` = progressive; `-ke-` = continuative; `-me-` = habitual; `-se-` = inchoative; `-te-` = opposite of inchoative. `-we-` remains unresolved.
9. **Converbs:** converbs are to be derived through nominalizers and case endings. The exact nominalizer inventory and case-to-function mapping remain open.
10. **Participles:** the participial system combines the existing stem grades with dedicated participial morphology. The exact dedicated forms remain open.

### Syntax

11. **Alignment:** mixed Split-S system conditioned by person and animacy: speech-act participants are strongly agentive; among third persons, animacy/volitionality conditions the alignment pattern. Exact conditioning remains open.
12. **Negation:** verbal morphology, with the exact marker and template position still open.
13. **Relative clauses:** participial relatives precede the noun; finite relative clauses follow the noun.
14. **Clause combining:** converbs and nominalized clauses are the primary strategy.

## Current status

The decisions above were explicitly made in conversation and have been recorded in the development branch. Remaining questions stay in `open-questions.md` rather than being inferred or silently resolved.
