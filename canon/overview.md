# Language Canon Overview

This directory contains established linguistic rules. Treat these files as authoritative unless a later canonical file explicitly supersedes them.

## Status conventions

- **ESTABLISHED** — decided and usable as canon.
- **TENTATIVE** — currently preferred, but not fully locked.
- **?** — unresolved.
- **—** — not applicable.

## Current design

Naturalistic a priori language with compact, consonant-heavy morphology, historical fusion/reduction, conditioned sound change, and inherited monoconsonantal roots.

## Synchrony vs. diachrony

Synchronically, the surface syllable is generally `(C)V(C)`. Complex-looking forms may result from morphological concatenation and subsequent historical fusion/reduction. Do not assume that every surface sequence corresponds to an independent synchronically productive consonant cluster.

## Core morphological principles

- Roots are historically monoconsonantal.
- Verbal stem grades occur at the final vowel of the stem.
- `-a-` is NONFINITE.
- `-e-` is LINKING/ATTRIBUTIVE.
- `-u-` is REALIS.
- `-i-` is IRREALIS.
- These grades are independent of directional `i-` and `a-`.
- There is one object slot.
- Plural is expressed by pronouns rather than verbal agreement.
- `-h-` is EXCLAMATIVE; `-y-` is INTERROGATIVE; `-w-` is unresolved.

## Current finite-verb template

`(DIRECTION) (OBJECT) STEM (AUX/DERIV) (APPLICATIVE) AGENT (DISCOURSE) TENSE (ASPECT)`

## Priority rule for development

Do not silently turn a hypothesis into canon. Record unresolved alternatives in `development/` and promote a decision to `canon/` only after explicit resolution.
