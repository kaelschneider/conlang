# Syntax Overview

## Status

Partially established. Core word order and several clause-level systems are established; some finer syntactic details remain unresolved.

## Basic word order

`SOV` — established by current examples such as `Neku kerande kuci.`

## Noun phrase

Attributive modifiers precede the noun: `sare kerande` = large.ATTR container.

Genitive position remains unresolved.

## Predicative statives

A stative predicate follows its subject: `Kerande sarui` = the container is large.

## Case and alignment

The system combines ABS/ERG argument marking with active/stative behavior. `-ku` marks an agentive/ERG participant; ABS is zero-marked.

The alignment is a mixed Split-S system conditioned by person and animacy: speech-act participants are strongly agentive; among third persons, animacy/volitionality conditions the alignment pattern.

## Pronouns

Pronouns can make person/number explicit, allowing verbal plural agreement to be absent. Established plural forms include `men` 1PL, `sen` 2PL, and `en` 3PL.

## Questions

Interrogation is marked morphologically by `-y-` in the verbal discourse slot. Example: `Seku kerande kutye?`

## Negation

Negation is expressed through verbal morphology. The exact negative marker(s) and their position within the verbal template remain to be specified.

## Relative clauses

Participial relatives precede the noun; finite relative clauses follow the noun.

## Clause combining

Converbs and nominalized clauses are the primary strategy for clause combining. Independent conjunctions are not currently established as the primary strategy.
